| Identity pill + actor stamping (BH-2515) | Any route: the top bar, `.topbar__right`, first pill (left of WATCHDOG) | One read on load, `GET /api/coord/whoami` → `{actor, access, email}`, answered by the coord Pages function itself (never forwarded to harness-jobs; still behind the `dash` session check, 403 without it). Pill copy per `access`: `on` → the email (`span.pill`); `off` (Pages env `CF_ACCESS_TEAM_DOMAIN` or `CF_ACCESS_AUD` unset) → `ACCESS OFF · writes as console`; `unverified` (env set, no verifiable `Cf-Access-Jwt-Assertion` matching `Cf-Access-Authenticated-User-Email`) → `IDENTITY UNVERIFIED · writes as console`; `unrecordable` (email outside `^[a-z0-9._@-]{1,96}$`) → `<email> · recorded as console`; all four warn states are `span.pill.pill--warn`; a failed read → `identity unknown`; before the read lands → `identity…` (`pill--muted`). The pill ellipsises at `max-width:40vw` so a long email cannot push the 375px bar into horizontal scroll; its `title` explains the state. Every admitted coord PUT/POST body and the call-guard DELETE query carry `actor` = that resolution (never the browser's value), and `POST /api/dash/action` bodies are stamped the same way; the box writes that actor on the `pause_board` / `pause_set` pauses row (`console` for anything outside the worker's actor charset).
