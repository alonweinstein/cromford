Every `bin/agent-call.sh <stage> <project> <model> …` call reads `stage_runner_for` in `bin/lib/model-route.sh`; the answer lands in `state/model-routes/stage-runner/stage.<stage>[@project].ans` (`-` = no route) and is reused for `AGENT_CALL_STAGE_ROUTE_TTL_S` (default 120 s). The two knobs and `AGENT_CALL_MODEL_MISMATCH` are `lib.agent-call.*` settings rows (`catalog/lanes.d/_lib.json`). Live probe on a box: run two calls on one stage inside 120 s and the `.ans` file's mtime does not move on the second one. alerts-medic: a `[medic] MODEL/RUNNER MISMATCH:` row gives a `held N own MODEL/RUNNER MISMATCH` line in `logs/medic.log` and no session. Verified by `tests/agent-call-stage-route-cache.bh-1837-test.sh` and `tests/alerts-medic-self-mismatch.bh-1837-test.sh` |
