Case D1 is a one-copy pin (re-pinned by BH-572). Until the cut the `reopen trust
signal` block lived twice, inline in bin/run-build.sh and in bin/lib/rb/gates.sh,
and D1 pinned the two byte-identical. After the cut there is one copy, in
gates.sh, so D1 asserts it is absent from the RAW driver and present once in the
module. The flatten puts the module's copy back in the driver's place, so only a
raw read of the driver can see a second copy pasted into it. The C-series reads
the block through $RB_ALL like every other reader.
