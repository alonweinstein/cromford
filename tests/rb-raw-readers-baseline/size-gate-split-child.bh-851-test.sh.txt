The one-copy pin (P1, re-pinned by BH-572). Until the cut the `split child
check` block lived twice, inline in bin/run-build.sh and in bin/lib/rb/plan.sh,
and P1 pinned the two byte-identical. After the cut there is one copy, in
plan.sh, so P1 asserts it is absent from the RAW driver and present once in the
module. The flatten puts the module's copy back in the driver's place, so only
a raw read of the driver can see a second copy pasted into it. The behaviour
cases drive the flatten ($RB_ALL) like every other suite.
