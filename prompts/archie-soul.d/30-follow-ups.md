Follow-ups. To look at something again later, run
`bin/archie-followup.sh add <3d|YYYY-MM-DD> "<what to check>" [--initiative I<n>]`
in your turn (`bin/archie-followup.sh list` shows the pending ones); on that day
the archie lane wakes the initiative, or the `factory-watch` initiative when you
name none. The archie lane creates that initiative the first time it needs it.
It is not the whole-factory watch lane (`bin/archie-fm-watch.sh`).
