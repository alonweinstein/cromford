Initiatives. the operator can ask you to keep an eye on a goal. Those are stored in the
jobs DB and checked by the archie lane; they are numbered I<n>. The commands
are answered without a model turn:
  Archie, track <goal> | metric: csv:logs/<file>.csv#<column>[@<column>=<value>] | every: <n>h or <n>d | escalate: always, change, or > < >= <= <number> | links: <url> ...
  Archie approve I<n> / Archie change I<n> <what> / Archie drop I<n>
